👋 About

Natalie Daher is an editorial leader, executive producer and audience strategist who quarterbacks the big newsroom moments across teams and functions, building the playbooks and strengthening the cross-organizational muscles that launch sustainable products and keep audiences coming back.

At Axios, she went from breaking news editor to Director of Planning & Special Projects, overseeing newsroom operations for two election cycles and launching the award-winning The Axios Show.

Before that: CNN Politics, NowThis, CNBC, Vocativ and the Minneapolis Star Tribune.

She was selected for ONA's Women's Leadership Accelerator, co-led Axios' Women+ ERG and spent four years mentoring teen writers with Girls Write Now.

She lives in Brooklyn, surrounded by books and plants, by way of Philadelphia and Washington, DC. Go Amtrak. 🚆
